Morning brief: three options

The PO chose option A on 24 November 2026. Its final form is the example of the handoff; these three pages stay as a record.

The same fictional day (Example Bank), the same look, three ways to organise the page. Each shows the day as one thread from the first hour to the last, the details of a block on a click, and the pending items as a second plan. Click a picture to open the option.

A · Agenda and side panel

The day is drawn to scale, like a calendar: a two-hour block is tall, a reminder is thin. The details of the selected block stay open beside it.

Details
A panel on the right, always open
Pending
A second tab
Best at
Walking the day block by block
Weak at
The pending items are one click away, out of sight

Before the briefing · Its pending tab

B · Day and pending side by side

The day is a list on the left, one line per block. The pending items never leave the screen, on the right. A click on a line opens its details in a pop-up beside it.

Details
A pop-up beside the line
Pending
A column always in view
Best at
Seeing everything without a click
Weak at
The densest of the three; the length of a block is a number, not a size

Before the briefing

C · Day ribbon and focus

The whole day is one ribbon across the page. Under it, the block of the moment in large, with the rest of the day beside it. The pending items are further down.

Details
In place, in large, under the ribbon
Pending
Down the page, by scrolling
Best at
Answering “what am I doing now?” at a glance
Weak at
A short block has no room for its title on the ribbon

Opened from here, each option follows the real clock during the day and shows 11:20 outside it. Add ?now=15:10 to an address to see another hour. Nothing on these pages is saved: each button copies a request for the agent.
