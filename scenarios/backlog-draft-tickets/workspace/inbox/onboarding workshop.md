# Onboarding workshop, {workday-1}

With the tech lead and the architect, about Mobile onboarding.

- A customer who stops halfway must be able to save the application and resume it later, on another device. The application is kept for 30 days, and the customer gets a link by mail to resume it.
- The customer scans an identity document during onboarding, and the scan is checked before the account opens.
- A customer who prefers to finish in a branch must find the nearest one: given a postcode, the app lists the three nearest branches with their opening hours.
