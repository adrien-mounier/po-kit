Morning page: the special states

Six states that the two examples do not show, each on the day the examples use. This is a catalogue, not one real page: a frame holds the bar and the part of the day that the state changes. Everything else stays as the examples draw it.

A block that is done

A block whose record is done, or that the agent marked done, is drawn as a past block whatever the hour, with the check sign and the word “Done” where its flag was. A past block that nobody reported done keeps its flag, faded.
